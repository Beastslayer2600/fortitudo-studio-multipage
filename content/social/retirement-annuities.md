## LinkedIn (long-form, professional)

Most people know an RA has something to do with tax.

Very few understand exactly how much — or what it costs them to get it wrong.

Here is what the structure actually does:

**At contribution:** Up to 27.5% of your taxable income is deductible. At a marginal rate of 36%, every R100 contributed costs you R64. That gap is immediate return before the investment has done anything.

**Inside the fund:** No income tax on interest. No dividends withholding tax. No capital gains tax. Every rand of return compounds without being partially surrendered to SARS each year.

**At retirement:** The first R550,000 lump sum is tax-free. The rest is used to purchase an annuity — and the structure of that annuity is one of the most consequential financial decisions most people ever make.

The lock-in to age 55 is not a flaw. It is the most valuable feature. You cannot undo it when the business has a bad year or a lifestyle upgrade feels justified. That forced discipline is worth more than most people realise.

The mistakes that cost people the most:
— Drawing down a living annuity above 6% in early retirement
— Never updating beneficiary nominations
— Treating the RA as the only retirement vehicle when a TFSA running alongside extracts more from the tax system

I have written a full breakdown — contribution mechanics, annuity structures, creditor protection, estate bypass, and the decisions that matter.
